ooooh

A voice-first thinking companion for people who think better by talking but hate speaking into silence.

Coming to the App Store

It listens while you think.

Start a session and talk for as long as you need. Your words are transcribed on the phone and the app keeps track of where you've been: the ideas, the decisions, the questions you left open. It doesn't interrupt.

It asks one question, only when it matters.

When you circle the same point, hesitate, or leave a thread hanging, it asks one short question out loud and goes quiet again. Most of the time it says nothing, because most of the time you don't need it to.

It writes the notes afterward.

When you stop, the session becomes notes: ideas, decisions, questions, insights, actions, open threads, and the lines worth keeping. They stay on your phone, and you can export them as Markdown whenever you like.
