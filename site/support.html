Support

Last updated October 27, 2026

ooooh is an iPhone app made by me, Sean Reid. You talk, it listens, and every so often it asks one short question. When you end the session it writes your notes. If something isn't working, write to hello@ooooh.app and I'll answer myself, usually within a day.

Getting started

Tap the button and start talking. The first time, the app asks for the microphone and for speech recognition; both run on the phone. Talk for a few minutes about whatever you're working through. The app stays quiet for the first 45 seconds, and after that it speaks only when it hears a real pause after something worth asking about.

Why it hasn't said anything

Most of the time, because it shouldn't. The app is built to ask rarely. In a ten minute session, five or six questions is a lot. If it has been quiet for a long stretch while you were silent, it will ask whether you're done, on screen, with a light tap. If the orb says offline, the phone can't reach my server; the app keeps listening and still writes your notes when you end.

Voice or quiet

With headphones in, questions are spoken. On the speaker, they appear on screen with a tap instead, so the app never talks over itself in a room. You can force either in Settings under Quiet mode.

Starting without looking

Say "Start ooooh" to Siri, or set the Action button to the Start shortcut. The phone has to be unlocked, which is Apple's rule for starting a recording. Once a session is running, the button on your headphones or your car's wheel pauses it and presses again to resume.

Your notes

Notes are editable. Tap Edit to change or delete an item, add one, or mark a thread resolved. Tap a note item to see the moment in the transcript it came from. The app's own questions appear in the transcript too; press and hold one to mark it as a bad question, which helps me make the next ones better. Export everything as Markdown from Settings.

Free sessions and the subscription

Three sessions a month are free, each with twenty minutes of questions; listening and notes continue past that. After the third, the app asks you to subscribe: $6.99 a month or $49.99 a year through the App Store. Apple handles billing and refunds; manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under Subscriptions.

iCloud

While your phone is signed in to iCloud, your sessions sync to your private iCloud database and come back on a new phone. Turn it off in Settings if you'd rather they stay on one phone. The privacy page says exactly what leaves your phone and what doesn't.

Deleting

Delete one session from History, or everything from Settings. Both remove the data from the phone, and from iCloud when sync is on.
